PVD model: atom by atom

Atoms leave a source and fly in straight lines (line-of-sight) to the substrate. Watch the top grow thick while the trench sidewalls stay thin.

Navy atoms are physically ejected or evaporated from the source; no chemical reaction is needed. Raise the angular spread or pressure to scatter more atoms into the trench; raise surface mobility to let atoms settle into a denser film. Sources: Mattox (2010); Ohring (2002); Thornton & Hoffman (1989). Illustrative 2D cross-section: 1 cell ≈ 1 nm. Not a quantitative process simulator.
